## 🛡️ About Me

I am a **Computer Science undergraduate** at **SASTRA University** ('27) with hands-on experience in **Security Operations (SOC)** and **Detection Engineering**, specializing in threat detection, telemetry pipelines, and incident response.

- 🔍 **Core Domain**: Security Operations (SOC), alert correlation, telemetry ingestion, and designing rule-based heuristics & behavioral analysis for rapid incident triage.
- ☁️ **Cloud Defense & IaC**: Expanding skills into Cloud Security to design and implement resilient defense across modern cloud environments with **AWS**, **Terraform (IaC)**, and container security.
- 🛠️ **Systems & Infrastructure**: Engineering native Windows endpoint telemetry sensors in **Rust**, scalable high-throughput backends in **FastAPI**, and analytical log architectures using **ClickHouse** & **PostgreSQL**.
- 🎓 **Academics & Leadership**: B.Tech CSE @ **SASTRA University** (*2023 - 2027*), serving as **Project Lead & Core Member** at the **Association of Computing Engineers (ACE)**.
